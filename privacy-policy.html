Last updated: 7 November 2026

Welcome to Delhi Press – Sajjad Printers (https://carddelhipress.in/), referred to in this policy as “we”, “our” or “us”. We respect your privacy and explain below what information may be collected when you use our website, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: details such as your name, email address, mobile number and login-related information when you create or use an account.
  • Order and enquiry information: details you submit about an order or enquiry, such as contact details, selected catalogue/design, delivery or communication details, and order status.
  • Reviews and feedback: ratings, comments and other feedback you choose to submit.
  • Website usage information: pages or catalogue items viewed, basic visit/count information, browser/device information, approximate location derived from technical information, and diagnostic data that may be provided by your browser or service providers.
  • Information you send us: any details you choose to provide when contacting us.

Please do not submit passwords, full payment-card details, government identity numbers or other sensitive information in review or enquiry text fields.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • create and manage user accounts and provide website features;
  • display catalogue items and respond to enquiries;
  • process, manage and communicate about orders;
  • display and manage ratings, reviews and feedback;
  • operate, secure, maintain and improve the website;
  • understand general website usage and prevent spam, fraud or misuse;
  • meet legal obligations and resolve disputes; and
  • where advertising is enabled, display, measure and improve advertisements in accordance with applicable settings and policies.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and service providers may use cookies, local storage, session storage or similar technologies to support sign-in sessions, remember preferences, maintain website functions, measure visits, improve security and, where enabled, support advertising.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies or storage technologies may affect sign-in or other website features.

These technologies may use information about your visit to this website or other websites to show relevant advertisements, subject to your settings and applicable law.

Google's advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this website and/or other websites. You can manage Google's ad personalisation settings here: Google Ads Settings. You can also learn how Google uses information on partner sites here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Third-party advertising providers may have their own privacy policies and practices. Please review those policies for details about their data use. We do not control cookies or tracking technologies set by third parties.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers when reasonably needed to operate the website and provide requested services. These may include:

  • website hosting, database and infrastructure providers, including Supabase where used by the website;

Service providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We aim to share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

No website, internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please keep your account credentials confidential and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide website services, manage accounts and orders, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information that you have provided, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You can also manage cookies through your browser and manage Google's personalised advertising settings through the link in Section 4.
